Our Standard Service Process

How our certified technicians perform doorstep servicing step-by-step.

01

1. Inspection & Testing

Technician checks electrical voltage, noise levels, motion sensor responses, and motor performance before starting work.

02

2. Full Disassembly

Carefully removing the baffle filters, oil collector, blower assembly, and housing to expose inner grease buildup.

03

3. Chemical Degreasing

Applying high-grade non-toxic degreaser solution to dissolve carbonized oil from fan blades and interior walls.

04

4. Reassembly & Airflow Check

Re-assembling components, lubricating motor rotor shaft, and testing final high CFM suction power.
